Owner: «طبق فقط الجولة ١ و ٢ و ٣ على HerZone.app». Round 4 is NOT part of this. Answers the same day:
- Where: the website AND the Flutter app.
- Texts: use the preview's wording as it is (the owner may edit later). Policy-like texts in the preview were examples,
  not the owner's policy, and are NOT copied: refund wording and deadlines, FAQ answers, what the fee includes, the
  waitlist promises. Those screens use the texts and rules the product already has, or wait for the owner.
- Welcome: nothing under the two buttons and no number above the headline (the Stripe idea of round 1 is dropped).
- Release: straight to herzone.app after tests and the usual release order (baseline checks recorded, CI green,
  push `release`, production deployment with the owner's Allow, smoke checks, Sentry).

Spec: `qatar/docs/design/preview-2026-11-14/herzone-preview.html` (the interactive preview the owner approved; its
screens are named in the `SCREENS` object, its motion and sizes are in its `<style>`). It is a mock-up, not product
code: build each idea in the real components, with tokens only (`qatar/design/tokens.json`), in Arabic and English.

## Batches (one at a time, each with tests, real-font check at 360/390/402/430, ar/en, light/dark)

1. Web, round 1: welcome photo (fade and settle) instead of the diagram; sections and cards rise in once (Apple);
   answers dim on press and fill on release (Framer); after an answer the question line gives way to one reply line
   and a seat fills, progress bar starts part-filled (Duolingo); home: three tinted tiles, the week strip as the only
   filter with a live count, this week's meetups as a photo peek row (Notion, Airbnb, MUJI); next-meetup card in a
   double frame with rows arriving one by one (Linear); /about with the four-size scale and quote-then-photo (Pentagram).
2. Web, round 2: details (giant date numeral, pinned summary on scroll, floating book pill with the product's own
   refund line docking above it); booking review as a receipt; inbox on a dated rail; getting-ready card as the empty
   state of "my meetups"; two-tone interstitial; feedback as three echoing rows; gliding tab indicator.
   Reminders picker: only if the product can really send those three reminders separately; otherwise report to the owner.
3. Web, round 3: desktop layout (two columns, reserved slots while loading); dark depth (one new surface step in
   tokens.json, contrast checked); full meetup → what happens next (only what the waitlist really does); account groups
   with the ✓ in the row; the 1–5 scale as one track; "your card" as a membership card with the cropped ring (the logo
   itself untouched); "what you get" as a plain roster; venue reveal as one place card; cancel as a when / what-you-get-back
   table from the real refund rules. Fee + FAQ page: waits for the owner's answers (the preview's were examples).
4. App (Flutter): the same ideas, batch by batch, in the app's own widgets.
5. Release.
