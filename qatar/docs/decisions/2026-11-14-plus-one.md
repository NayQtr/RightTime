# "+1": a member may bring one companion

**Date:** 14 Nov 2026. **Status:** owner's decision; built on `rightime-app`, not released. The new Arabic lines wait
for the owner's review before release.

**Context.** The owner asked for a way to come to a meetup with someone she knows. HerZone is women only.

**Decision (the owner's).**
- The option is a control labelled «+1» in the booking flow. Pressing it shows «هذا الخيار يتيح لكِ الحضور برفقة من تودّين
  حضورها.»
- The member writes the companion's **name only**: no phone number, no account.
- The amount doubles: two organising fees.
- The companion takes a second seat at the **same table**.
- Cancelling cancels **both seats together** under the same refund deadline: one booking, one payment, one refund of
  the whole amount.

**How it is built.**
- Migration `qatar/db/migrations/026_plus_one.sql`: `bookings.seats` (1 or 2) and `bookings.guest_name` (NULL exactly
  when `seats = 1`, at most 60 characters). `bookings.price_minor` is the total (fee × seats); the API also returns
  `unit_fee_minor`.
- API: `POST /api/v1/bookings` takes `guest: { name }`; `409 NOT_ENOUGH_SEATS` when two seats are not free,
  `422 GUEST_NAME_REQUIRED` for an empty or too long name. Every booking object carries `seats`, `guest_name` and
  `unit_fee_minor`; sessions and the review data carry `plus_one_available`.
- `POST /api/v1/bookings/{id}/guest` (`{ guest: { name } }` or `{ guest: null }`) adds, renames or removes the
  companion of an unpaid booking while its hold runs. The website needs it because the seat is held when the session
  is chosen and «+1» is on the next step (review). The app may use it the same way; the booking call is unchanged.
- Payment: the server sets the amount (fee × seats). A charge of any other amount (an older payment page left open)
  never confirms the booking and is refunded in full.
- Website: the «+1» toggle on the review step, the companion's name (required) and a required check box («أؤكد أن
  مرافقتي امرأة وعمرها 18 سنة أو أكثر.»). The second seat is taken when she presses pay.

**What counts seats, not bookings.**
- `event_sessions.seats_sold`: the hold (`hold_seat`), the review-step change (`set_booking_guest`), expired holds
  (`release_expired_holds`), every cancellation and failed payment (`release_seat_on_cancel`), rebooking after an
  organiser cancellation (`rebook_booking`, which keeps the companion and needs two free seats).
- Waitlist offers stay one seat per member: two freed seats are offered to the first two members waiting. A member
  with a kept seat can add a companion only if a second seat is free.
- `event_tables.confirmed_count` (`refresh_table_count`), so the quorum of 4, the lock, the table figure she sees and
  the coordinators' board count the companion; `move_booking_table` needs room for both.
- Matching (`engine.js` and `matching_engine.py`): the companion has no answers; one booking is never split, and its
  two seats count toward the table size of 4 to 6. Scores are computed from the members' answers only.
- Admin console: seats sold, paid and waiting, holds, the pool by language and budget, seated in a matching run,
  checked in and late cancellations on the board. The companion's name shows beside her member.
- Venue page: the number of women at a table, and «+1 name» on the member's row. Marking the member as arrived counts
  both (there is no separate arrival for the companion).
- Not counted from bookings: the number of guests a venue is asked for (`session_venues.planned_guests`), which the
  coordinator types.

**Promo codes and the launch price.** Promo codes are only checked and shown on the plans page; nothing is redeemed
against a booking, so two seats change nothing there. The launch switch (`PRICING_LAUNCH_FREE`) only changes what is
shown: the amount is still taken. With «+1» the amount taken and shown struck through is the total for two seats.

**Personal data.** The companion's name is in the member's data export (`companion_name`) and is erased when the
account is deleted (kept as an empty text so past seat counts stay right). It is listed in the data inventory of
`qatar/docs/legal/dpia-draft.md`. Replies to booking calls are kept for 24 hours for safe retries (as every write),
and they include the name.

**Open points for the owner.**
1. The new Arabic lines (the control, the field label, the check box, the two receipt rows, "not available", the cancel
   note, and «اكتبي اسم مرافقتك.» for an empty name, which is Claude's wording) need the owner's review.
2. The privacy notice has no line yet about the companion's name. The companion herself does not consent: the member
   gives her name.
3. The women at the table see each other's first names on the day. The companion's name is not shown to them today;
   only the table's count includes her. Show it or not?
4. The companion is not checked against a payment card (the women-only check uses the member's card). The check box
   is the only confirmation.
5. Celebrations, the age rule and "not the same two women again" use the member's answers only; the companion is
   outside them.
6. After the full-refund deadline the existing rule applies to the whole amount (both fees).

**Revisit** when the owner answers the points above, and before a real payment provider is connected (the amount of an
open payment must be updated at the provider when the companion is added or removed).
