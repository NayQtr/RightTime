Frequently asked

Is HerZone a dating app?

No. HerZone is for social meetups and friendships between women around a table, and we don't promote romantic expectations.

How is my table formed?

We seat you with women who share your values, personality and interests, in your language and within your budget.

What if I want to meet someone again?

After the event you can choose "I'd like to stay in touch". Contact opens only if both people choose it.

What if the event doesn't feel right?

Contact the coordinator on duty at any time. Private reporting is always available and stays confidential.
